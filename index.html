Md. Mahmodul Hasan

I am a Lecturer in Software Engineering at the University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh. My work combines federated learning, differential privacy and explainable AI to build trustworthy diagnostic systems that can be trained across hospitals without sharing patient data. I hold an M.Sc. (Engg.) and a B.Sc. (Engg.) in CSE from MBSTU, ranking first in both, and I am seeking a PhD position to deepen this research.

Download CV Get in touch

Research Interests

Federated Learning & Privacy-Preserving MLMedical Image ProcessingExplainable AIAdversarial Machine LearningMobile Robots & Computer Vision

My recent flagship result applies federated learning with differential privacy to polyp segmentation (Smart Health, 2025). Earlier work introduced fuzzy pooling CNNs with explainable AI for lung ultrasound (Computers in Biology and Medicine, 2023). I am interested in extending this line toward robustness against adversarial and inference attacks, and clinically meaningful explanations in federated settings.

Publications

Journal articles, conference papers and a book chapter, each linked by DOI. Full list on Google Scholar and ResearchGate.

    Ongoing Projects

    • Polyp classification with liquid neural networks: robust classification of gastrointestinal polyps from endoscopic images.
    • Cardiac arrhythmia detection: deep learning for shockable and non-shockable rhythms from ECG signals.
    • Pull request outcome prediction: attention-driven explainable AI to predict whether a PR is merged, revised or closed.
    • Automated crack detection: computer vision for structural cracks in buildings.
